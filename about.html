About Moni's Hot

Our Story

Moni's Hot started with one simple idea: BBQ should taste the way it does at a family gathering — smoky, juicy and full of real spices. Every tikka, boti and kabab is marinated fresh and cooked over charcoal, then packed hot and sent to your door anywhere in Karachi.

We keep the menu focused on what we do best, so each dish gets the same care whether you order one plate or feed the whole family.

Fresh Ingredients

Fresh meat and authentic spices, prepared daily. Nothing is kept for tomorrow.

Real Charcoal Grill

Cooked over live charcoal for that signature smoky flavour you cannot fake.

Free Delivery

Free delivery across Karachi, with live order tracking right after you check out.
