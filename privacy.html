Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we collect it and how we look after it.

Last updated: November 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This page explains what information Moni's Hot collects, how we use it and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and password when you sign up.
  • Order details: your phone number, delivery address, the items you order and your chosen payment method.
  • Messages: anything you send us through the contact form.
  • Basic device data: such as browser type, used to keep the website working properly.

2. How We Use It

  • To prepare, deliver and track your orders.
  • To manage your account and sign you in.
  • To reply to your questions and support requests.
  • To improve our menu, website and service.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

This website stores some data in your browser, such as your cart, your login session and your light or dark mode choice. This keeps the site working the way you expect. You can clear it any time from your browser settings, although your cart and login session will be reset.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only when needed to complete your order, such as with delivery riders and payment providers, or when the law requires it.

5. Payments

Card, JazzCash and EasyPaisa payments are handled by the payment provider. We do not store your full card details on this website.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no website or transmission method is completely secure. Please choose a strong password and keep it private.

7. Your Choices

You can ask us to view, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting us.

8. Children

Our service is not directed at young children, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.

10. Contact

Privacy questions? Reach us through our Contact page.
